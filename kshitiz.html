Lower Body Power

Glutes, hamstrings & unilateral leg strength — built for a 45-year-old intermediate lifter, advanced runner, and HYROX beginner training with dumbbells and resistance tubes.

Client: Kshitiz Mahajan Coach: Sushant 45 yrs Intermediate lifter Advanced runner HYROX beginner Dumbbells + tubes 4 days / week

Before every lower-body session

Weak glutes usually means inhibited, not just untrained — this wakes them up before you load them. Also do this before hard runs, 5–7 min.

  1. Banded lateral walks 2 × 15 steps / side
  2. Banded clamshells 2 × 15 / side
  3. Glute bridge march 2 × 10 / side
  4. Bird-dog 2 × 8 / side

8-week progression scheme

WeeksLever to progressHow
1–2TempoSlower eccentrics (3-sec down), add pauses at end range
3–4RepsAdd 1–2 reps per set at the same load
5–6LoadMove up to the next dumbbell pair
