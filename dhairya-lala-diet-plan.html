🌿 Daily Detox Drink

Morning, empty stomach

Dhania–Jeera–Saunf Water — a coriander, cumin and fennel infusion. Together these seeds support digestion, help regulate blood pressure and blood sugar, and are commonly used as a gentle metabolism and immunity booster.

Ingredients: 1 tsp coriander (dhania) seeds, 1 tsp cumin (jeera) seeds, 1 tsp fennel (saunf) seeds, 1 large glass water.

Method:

  1. Add all three seeds to a glass of water at night.
  2. Let it soak overnight.
  3. Next morning, bring it to a boil.
  4. Strain and drink once cooled.

Keep it plain per your plan — skip the pink salt/lemon add-on some recipes suggest, since spices and lemon are off the list for now.
