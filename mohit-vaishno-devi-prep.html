Program Overview

Coach: Sushant FMS | Physio-Led Fitness

This 9-day specialized program prepares you for the Vaishno Devi Yatra (\~12-13 km steep inclines + stairs, \~1700m elevation gain at moderate altitude).

Primary Focus:
• Leg Endurance (Quads • Hamstrings • Glutes • Calves)
• Knee Strength & Joint Stability
• Cardio-Lung Capacity for sustained high-altitude effort

Equipment: Leg Extension • Leg Curl • Leg Press • Smith Machine • Dumbbells • Barbell

General Guidelines (Follow Daily)

  • Warm-up (8-10 mins): 5 mins light treadmill/bike + dynamic mobility (squats, leg swings, lunges)
  • Form First: Full range, 3-sec controlled lowering on extensions & curls
  • Progression: Last 2-3 reps challenging with perfect form
  • Rest: 60-90 sec (shorter on endurance days)
  • Cardio: Incline treadmill walk = best yatra simulation
  • Cool-down: 5 mins static stretches (quads, hams, calves, hip flexors)
  • Recovery: 7-8 hrs sleep, protein + carbs post-workout, stay hydrated
