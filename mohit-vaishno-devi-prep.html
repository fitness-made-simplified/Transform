Program Overview

Coach: Sushant FMS | Physio-Led Fitness

This 9-day specialized program prepares you for the Vaishno Devi Yatra (\~12-13 km steep inclines + stairs, \~1700m elevation gain at moderate altitude). Primary focus of this protocol is practical trekking performance — not muscle gain or aesthetics.

Primary Focus:
• Leg Endurance (Quads • Hamstrings • Glutes • Calves)
• Knee Strength & Joint Stability
• Cardio-Lung Capacity for sustained high-altitude effort

Equipment: Leg Press • Smith Machine • Dumbbells • Bodyweight

General Guidelines (Follow Daily)

  • Warm-up (8-10 mins): 5 mins light treadmill/bike + dynamic mobility (squats, leg swings, lunges)
  • Form First: Full range, controlled tempo, knees tracking over toes
  • Progression: Focus on quality movement and breathing
  • Rest: 60-90 sec between sets (minimal on circuits)
  • Cardio: Incline treadmill & stair climb = best yatra simulation
  • Cool-down: 5 mins static stretches (quads, hams, calves, hip flexors)
  • Recovery: 7-8 hrs sleep, protein + carbs post workout, stay hydrated
