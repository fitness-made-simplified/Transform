VAISHNO DEVI
TREK PREP

9-Day Protocol · Mohit · Age 40
9Days
~15Trek KMs
5200Ft Altitude
3Focus Pillars

Mohit, this 9-day program is precision-built around your Vaishno Devi Yatra — a 15 km steep incline trek with stairs at high altitude. The goal isn't aesthetics; it's functional readiness. Each day builds on the last across three pillars: knee resilience, leg muscular endurance, and cardio-pulmonary capacity. By Day 9, your body will be primed to handle the ascent comfortably and safely.

Mohit, at 40 with moderate training history, your body is fully capable of handling this yatra — but preparation is everything. This 9-day block addresses the three things that make or break trekkers at altitude: knee control on descent, leg muscular endurance over distance, and cardio efficiency with reduced O₂. Follow each day in sequence. Don't skip Day 4 recovery or Day 9 rest. The mountain will test your lungs and legs equally — now they're ready for it. Train smart. Trek strong. Jai Mata Di. 🏔️
